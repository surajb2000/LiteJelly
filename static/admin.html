    Metadata cache

    Answers are kept on disk so a scan never waits on a third party. Clear a show when it was matched to the wrong title.

    Playback

    Turn this on only if your TV decodes HEVC. When off, HEVC is re-encoded to H.264, which costs CPU but plays anywhere.

    Never upscales; smaller sources keep their own size. Viewers can still pick a different quality while watching.

    Scrub previews

    One sheet of small frames is built per file the first time it is played, in the background. Measured on a desktop: about seven seconds and 224 KB for a 24 minute episode.

    Smaller means a finer preview and a larger sheet. Existing sheets are rebuilt on next play after a change.
